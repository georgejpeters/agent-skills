Pass only if the answer identifies ALL THREE of these specific problems (wording may differ):
1. DEFINITION MISMATCH: the provider's "on target" flag can count events the bookmaker's Opta
   settlement does not (e.g. shots blocked by an outfield player), so the model is graded on a
   different, easier event than the one it bets on.
2. LOOK-AHEAD IN THE UNIVERSE: "60+ minutes played" is only known after the match; the universe
   must be defined by information available before kick-off (e.g. the confirmed starting XI).
3. PRICE TIMING: "the last snapshot stored" may be an in-play or post-kick-off price; the test must
   use the last pre-kick-off price that could actually have been taken.
Generic advice (sample size, overfitting) does not substitute for any of the three.
