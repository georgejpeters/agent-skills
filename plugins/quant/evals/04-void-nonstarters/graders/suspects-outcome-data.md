Pass only if the answer raises that the settlement data itself may be contaminated, specifically
that it can include players who did not start or did not play (non-starters / unused subs,
voided or lapsed markets), which deflates the observed rate. It must recommend filtering the
outcomes to players who actually started/played (or to the same population the model predicts)
before judging calibration. Generic "check your data" does not pass.
